** 'Illegal Instruction' - Bad Encoding, CSR privilege violation, mret in User Mode
* Raising a trap
** A trap will be raised with some additional information, providing a value for the mcause and mtval
** Starting the trap process
*** Swap mscratch(which contains the address to the start of the trapframe) and sp(x2)
*** mscratch now contains the value of sp(x2)
*** Save all register x1 through x31(apart from x2) onto the trapframe
*** Save the mscratch value(sp) onto the trapframe as well
*** Save mepc onto the trapframe
*** Change sp to the point just beyond all these saves
